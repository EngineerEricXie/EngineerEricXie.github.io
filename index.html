02 / Engineering

Featured build

Open source · C++ / CUDA

GPU-accelerated isogeometric analysis for flow and transport

A validated simulation pipeline for flow and transport in complex branching geometries. It turns neuron reconstructions into physics-grounded, scalable data generation for scientific machine learning and digital-twin work.

Across three branching-neuron cases the V100 backend finishes the reported end-to-end runs in 7.87–78.52 s, 36.0×–154.5× faster than the legacy NeuronTransportIGA CPU implementation.

03 / Publications

Selected papers

First-author work in bold. The full, automatically updated list is on Google Scholar.

  1. arXiv2026 · preprint

    MGRD: Compact morphology-gated residual diffusion for variance-aware cross-domain neurite forecasting

    T.Y. Hsieh, C. Anitescu, C. Kim, V.A. Webster-Wood, Y.J. Zhang
    arXiv preprint, 2026

    Forecasts 20 future neurite-morphology frames from 10 observations, with repeated sampling to estimate forecast variability. Reduces trajectory-wise mean MAE by 39.6% and improves skeleton F1 by 45.3% over gSTA on human iPSC-derived neuron microscopy, using 1.01% of its parameters.

    arXiv
  2. bioRxiv2026 · preprint

    Predicting macroscopic axon topology from microscopic kinematics: an interactive tracking and random walk pipeline for substrate-dependent cortical neurospheres

    C. Kim, M. Kim, H. Cao, T.Y. Hsieh, Y.J. Zhang, T. Cohen-Karni, V. Webster-Wood
    bioRxiv, 2026

    Links single-axon growth dynamics to network-scale topology; the generative model reproduces both microscopic behavior and macroscopic axonal density.

    bioRxiv
